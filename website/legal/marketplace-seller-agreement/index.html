Marketplace Seller Agreement

Last Updated: March 2026

This applies to businesses selling sports equipment through the Athlete’s POV Marketplace.

On this page

  1. 1. Product Listings
  2. 2. Pricing and Inventory
  3. 3. Order Fulfilment
  4. 4. Commission
  5. 5. Returns and Refunds

1. Product Listings

Sellers may list products such as:

  • Sports equipment
  • Apparel
  • Training gear
  • Accessories

All product descriptions must be accurate and truthful.

2. Pricing and Inventory

Sellers must ensure:

  • Correct pricing
  • Updated stock availability
  • Genuine products

Counterfeit products are strictly prohibited.

3. Order Fulfilment

Sellers must:

  • Ship products within the promised timeframe
  • Provide order tracking when applicable
  • Handle product warranty or support issues

4. Commission

Athlete’s POV may charge:

  • A sales commission
  • Platform service fees

Commission terms will be defined in the seller onboarding agreement.

5. Returns and Refunds

Sellers must comply with Athlete’s POV Return & Refund Policy. See the Cancellation & Refund Policy.
