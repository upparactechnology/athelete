Athlete Representation Agreement

Last Updated: March 2026

This Athlete Representation Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between Athlete’s POV (“Company”) and the Athlete (“Client”) who registers for representation services on the Athlete’s POV platform.

1. Scope of Representation

Athlete’s POV may assist athletes with:

  • Brand partnerships and sponsorship opportunities
  • Talent representation and endorsements
  • Career guidance and consultancy
  • Tournament and event opportunities
  • Sports marketing and personal branding

Athlete’s POV will act as a representative and facilitator, connecting athletes with relevant opportunities.

2. Non-Exclusive Representation

Unless otherwise agreed in writing:

  • The representation provided by Athlete’s POV is non-exclusive.
  • Athletes may engage with other agents, brands, or agencies independently.

3. Commission Structure

Athlete’s POV may charge a representation commission on deals secured through the platform.

Typical commission range: 10%–25% of the contract value, depending on the type of opportunity. Exact commission terms will be communicated before any deal is finalized.

4. Athlete Responsibilities

The athlete agrees to:

  • Provide accurate personal and performance information
  • Maintain professional conduct
  • Fulfil obligations related to sponsorship or promotional deals
  • Comply with event and competition regulations

5. Payments

Payments for sponsorships or endorsements may be:

  • Processed through Athlete’s POV, or
  • Paid directly to the athlete depending on agreement terms.

Athlete’s POV may deduct agreed commissions before transferring payments.

6. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement at any time by providing written notice. Ongoing deals already signed will remain valid until completion.

7. Liability

Athlete’s POV does not guarantee:

  • Sponsorship deals
  • Tournament selections
  • Brand partnerships

All opportunities depend on athlete performance, market demand, and brand decisions.
