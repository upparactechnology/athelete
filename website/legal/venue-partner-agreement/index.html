Venue Partner Agreement

Last Updated: March 2026

This agreement governs the relationship between Athlete’s POV and sports venues listing their facilities on the platform.

1. Partnership Scope

Venue partners agree to:

  • List their sports facilities on Athlete’s POV
  • Provide accurate details about pricing, availability, and facilities
  • Honor bookings made through the platform

2. Commission Structure

Athlete’s POV may charge a commission fee on bookings processed through the platform. The commission percentage will be agreed upon separately between Athlete’s POV and the venue partner.

3. Booking Management

Venue partners must:

  • Maintain updated availability schedules
  • Accept valid bookings made through the platform
  • Provide the booked facility at the scheduled time

Failure to honour bookings may result in penalties or removal from the platform.

4. Venue Responsibilities

Venue partners are responsible for:

  • Maintaining safe and playable facilities
  • Providing accurate information on the platform
  • Ensuring staff availability during booking times

5. Payments

Payments for bookings may be processed by Athlete’s POV. Venue partners will receive payouts according to the agreed payment cycle (for example weekly or biweekly).

6. Liability

Venue partners are responsible for:

  • Player safety at their facilities
  • Compliance with local regulations
  • Insurance coverage if required

Athlete’s POV acts only as a technology platform facilitating bookings.

7. Termination

Either party may terminate the partnership with written notice. Athlete’s POV may suspend venue listings in cases of:

  • Repeated booking issues
  • Poor facility conditions
  • Policy violations

8. Policy Updates

Athlete’s POV reserves the right to update partnership terms. Policies can change without notice.
