Coach & Academy Partner Agreement

Last Updated: March 2026

This agreement governs partnerships between Athlete’s POV and sports coaches, academies, and training providers.

On this page

  1. 1. Service Listing
  2. 2. Accuracy of Information
  3. 3. Commission
  4. 4. Professional Conduct
  5. 5. Booking Management

1. Service Listing

Coaches and academies may list:

  • Training programs
  • Coaching sessions
  • Sports camps
  • Workshops

on the Athlete’s POV platform.

2. Accuracy of Information

Partners must ensure:

  • Training details are accurate
  • Pricing is transparent
  • Session availability is updated regularly

3. Commission

Athlete’s POV may charge a platform service fee or commission on coaching bookings. Commission structure will be agreed before listing.

4. Professional Conduct

Coaches and academies must:

  • Maintain professional training standards
  • Ensure player safety
  • Follow local regulations and sports guidelines

5. Booking Management

Partners must honour bookings made through the platform. Failure to provide booked sessions may result in:

  • Refunds to users
  • Removal from the platform
