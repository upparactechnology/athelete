Community Guidelines

Last Updated: March 2026

To maintain a safe and respectful sports environment, all users of Athlete’s POV are expected to follow these guidelines.

On this page

  1. 1. Expected Behaviour
  2. 2. Enforcement

1. Expected Behaviour

Users must:

  • Respect other players
  • Follow venue rules
  • Maintain sportsmanship
  • Avoid harassment or abusive behaviour

2. Enforcement

Violations may result in:

  • Account suspension
  • Permanent bans
  • Removal from events
