Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Athlete’s POV respects user privacy and is committed to protecting personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how user information is collected, used, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Date of birth
  • Profile information

Booking Information

  • Venue bookings
  • Event registrations
  • Payment records

Device Information

  • IP address
  • Device type
  • App usage data

2. How We Use Information

User information is used to:

  • Process bookings and payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve the platform
  • Send booking confirmations and updates
  • Notify users about new features or promotions

3. Sharing of Information

Athlete’s POV may share limited information with:

  • Venue partners
  • Event organizers
  • Payment processors
  • Technology service providers

User data will not be sold to third parties.

4. Data Security

Athlete’s POV uses industry-standard security measures to protect user information. However, no digital platform can guarantee 100% security.

5. User Rights

Users may:

  • Request access to their data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of their account

Requests can be made by contacting support.

Account Deletion

Partner users can delete their account directly in the Partner App: open Profile, choose Delete Partner Account, and confirm the deletion. Deletion always uses the authenticated (logged-in) account, so only your own account can be deleted.

When an account is deleted, Athlete’s POV anonymizes and disables the account identity information (such as name, email address, phone number, and login credentials) rather than hard-deleting historical business records. Venues belonging to the account are unlisted so they can no longer be booked.

Historical records such as bookings, transactions, settlements, disputes, and reviews may be retained where necessary for financial, operational, audit, security, or dispute purposes.

Users who cannot access their account can contact support at contact@athletespov.com. See also the Delete Account page.

6. Cookies and Analytics

The platform may use cookies and analytics tools to understand user behaviour and improve services.

7. Children’s Privacy

Athlete’s POV does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age without parental consent.

8. Policy Changes

Athlete’s POV may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Policies can change without notice.
