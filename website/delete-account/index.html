Delete Your Account

Last Updated: March 2026

This page explains how to delete an Athlete’s POV Partner account, what happens to your data when you do, and how to get help if you cannot access the app.

On this page

  1. 1. How to Delete Your Account
  2. 2. What Happens When You Delete
  3. 3. If You Cannot Access the App

1. How to Delete Your Account

Partner account deletion is done inside the Athlete’s POV Partner App:

  1. Open the Partner App and log in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Choose Delete Partner Account.
  4. Read the warning and choose Confirm Delete.
Authenticated deletion only. Deletion always uses the logged-in account, so only your own account can be deleted. You cannot delete another person’s account, and nobody can delete your account without logging in as you.

After confirmation you are signed out and returned to the login screen. Your login no longer works.

2. What Happens When You Delete

Anonymized and disabled

  • Your account identity information — such as name, email address, phone number, and login credentials — is anonymized and disabled.
  • Your login sessions are revoked, so the account can no longer be used.

Venues are unlisted

Venues belonging to your account are unlisted so they can no longer be discovered or booked. Venue, slot, and booking history is not physically erased as part of this step.

Records that may be retained

Historical records such as bookings, transactions, settlements, disputes, reviews, and audit records may be retained where necessary for financial, operational, audit, security, or dispute purposes.

Related: see User Rights in the Privacy Policy for your rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your data.

3. If You Cannot Access the App

If you cannot log in to delete your account yourself — for example, you lost access to your email address — contact our support team and we will help you:

Email: contact@athletespov.com Help Centre: Available within the app

Please write from your registered email address and include the phone number or email linked to your Partner account so we can verify ownership before taking action.
