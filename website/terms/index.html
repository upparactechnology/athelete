1. Platform Overview

Athlete’s POV provides a digital platform that allows users to:

  • Discover and book sports venues

  • Purchase sports-related products through the marketplace

Athlete’s POV acts as a facilitator between users and service providers and does not own or operate most venues or services listed on the platform.
